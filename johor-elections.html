PRN Johor (March 2022) vs GE15 (November 2022)

How DUN state seats under each parliamentary constituency voted in the Johor state election, and how those same voters swung eight months later in the federal election

PRN Johor: 12 March 2022 GE15: 19 November 2022 56 DUN seats · 26 Parliament seats 8 months apart
Key Findings
📊
BN landslide in PRN, reversed in GE15. BN won 40/56 DUN seats in March 2022 with a 2/3 majority. Eight months later in the same constituencies, PH won 15/26 parliamentary seats — a complete reversal of the narrative.
🔄
Massive swing to PH at federal level. Parliamentary seats like Iskandar Puteri (Liew Chin Tong, 60K+ majority), Pulai (Salahuddin, 33K), and Bakri flipped dramatically from their underlying BN-dominant DUN seats — showing voters split their ballots heavily.
🗳
Turnout surge. PRN Johor saw only 54.9% turnout — the lowest for any Johor state election. GE15 turnout in the same state shot up to 72.7%, bringing in ~450,000 additional voters who likely skewed strongly toward PH/urban candidates.
🏡
Urban–rural split. BN held its DUN seats in rural constituencies (Segamat, Mersing, Tenggara) and these translated into BN-won parliament seats too. But every urban/semi-urban parliamentary seat (JB, Iskandar Puteri, Johor Bahru, Kulai) went to PH in GE15 despite BN winning most of their DUNs in PRN.
⚠️
PN underperformed in both. PN won only 3 DUN seats in PRN (Bukit Kepong, Endau, Sedili area) and just 2 parliament seats in GE15 (Mersing, Kota Tinggi area). Johor is not PN territory — the BN vs PH contest dominates the state.
🔀
MUDA's one DUN seat (Puteri Wangsa) was a signal of urban youth dissatisfaction. Its underlying parliamentary seat (Tebrau/Pasir Gudang) became PH-friendly in GE15. MUDA's presence in PRN fragmented the anti-BN vote but didn't stop the eventual GE15 PH wave.

The 17.7% turnout gap between PRN and GE15 translates to ~450,000 extra voters at GE15 — mostly urban PH supporters who stayed home in March.

This single factor explains most of the apparent "reversal" between the two elections.
